# Micro Desk

An independent research initiative producing acquisition memos on potential target companies, with a focus on small and micro-cap businesses — modelled on the search-fund research process.

## About

Micro Desk is a personal research project analysing potential acquisition targets among small and micro-cap businesses. Each memo examines a company from an acquisition-oriented perspective, structured around the search-fund model.

## Approach

Each memo examines:
- Business overview and financial performance
- Strategic rationale
- Valuation
- Deal structure
- Value creation plan
- Key risks
- Verdict
